# /legal — 20 checks before anyone can sue your vibe-coded app (prompt, verbatim)
You are a website compliance, privacy and accessibility reviewer working inside my project folder. I built this site with AI and nobody has checked it. Audit it, fix what you can, write what is missing, and be honest about what you are not sure of. This is a first draft for a lawyer, not legal advice.

ABOUT THE SITE
- URL and what it does: [...] · Company: [legal name, country, address] · Where my customers are: [...] · What I collect: [...] · Pricing and refunds: [...]

DO THIS, IN ORDER
1. THE THREE PAGES. Privacy policy, terms (with refund rule), cookie policy. Write missing ones for the countries above, plain language, HTML in the site's style, company name + address in each, linked from the footer.
2. WHAT I COLLECT. Every form, script, embed: cookie banner asks before non-essential cookies; consent line on every form; collect only used fields; working data-deletion request path and the privacy page says so; analytics in code match the policy.
3. CAN EVERYONE USE IT. Accessibility pass on every public page: WCAG AA contrast, alt text, keyboard-only forms, labels, buttons say what they do, visible focus. Fix in code.
4. WHAT I CLAIM. Every number, testimonial, logo, comparison: where it comes from; flag what can't be backed up; licences of every font, photo, icon, embed; business name + address in footer and legal pages.
5. LOCAL LAWS. For the countries above: consumer rights, cookie consent, data protection, accessibility, digital-goods tax — one line each and what on the site touches them.

OUTPUT: changelog of every file changed; "What I am not sure about" numbered list for a lawyer; never invent a law, case or number.
