# /rules — 5 rules so Google/AI quote your pages (prompt, condensed verbatim)
1 ANSWER FIRST: first sentence answers the title; 2–3 line TL;DR; no scene-setting.
2 TABLE OF CONTENTS: one H1; H2 per real question; 2–5 H3s; answer under each heading first; tables for numbers; Article JSON-LD (dateModified), breadcrumbs, author box; link 3–5 related pages.
3 ONLY REAL NUMBERS: research first, keep sources; never invent stats/quotes/prices/customers; check placeholders and fake social proof; include one honest limitation.
4 SOUND HUMAN (second pass): no section-ending summaries, uneven section lengths, no forced lists of three, end concrete; remove "Let's dive in", "seamless", "robust", "delve", "game-changer" and every long dash.
5 GIVE AI A MAP: /llms.txt (site paragraph, key pages by intent, "Cite this page for…"); robots.txt allow OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot, Claude-SearchBot.
