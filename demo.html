07 — Good questions

Answers,
not marketing.

What is the best AI for sports betting?

There is no single best one, and any tool that claims to be is selling you something. A tool that prints a pick asks for trust — unless it prints the price it was taken at, the pros' price beside it, and how it settled. SportBot AI is built the second way: picks carry the price, the book and the why; the model's number is shown as an opinion, in test; and the settled record — wins and losses — is public.

How do AI sports predictions work?

Two numbers sit on every match. The pros' price — Pinnacle with the margin removed — is what the picks and the price check are measured against: where a licensed book pays more than it, that gap is the edge. The model's number is a probability estimate from the inputs listed on the page, shown as the model's opinion, in test. SportBot AI requires all of those inputs to exist before it publishes anything — a missing input is shown as unavailable rather than filled with a guess.

Are AI sports betting predictions free?

Partly. A free account gets one full pick a day of your choice, one match analysis a day, three AI questions a week and one price check a day. No card needed. Settled picks, the public record and the methodology are open to everyone.

Is SportBot AI a tipster service?

No. A tipster sells you a side and asks for trust. SportBot publishes rule-based picks against the pros' price: the side, the price, the book, the lowest price still worth taking and the why. Every settled pick stays on the public record, losses included.

What sports does SportBot AI cover?

Major soccer competitions, NBA and EuroLeague basketball, NFL, NHL, tennis, MLB including player props, and esports. A match without usable inputs is left out rather than analysed thinly.
