06 — Good questions
■ 01Is SportBot AI a tipster service?+

No. A tipster sells you a side and asks for trust. SportBot publishes rule-based picks against the pros' price — side, price, book, the lowest price still worth taking, and the why. Every settled pick stays on the public record, losses included.

■ 02How do the predictions work?+

Two numbers sit on every match: the pros' price (Pinnacle with the margin removed) and the model's number, shown as an opinion, in test. Where a licensed book pays more than the pros' price, that gap is the edge. Missing inputs are shown as unavailable, never guessed.

■ 03What is free?+

One full pick a day of your choice, one match analysis, one price check, three AI questions a week. The public record and the methodology are open to everyone.

■ 04What sports are covered?+

Major soccer competitions, NBA and EuroLeague, NFL, NHL, tennis, MLB including player props, and esports. A match without usable inputs is left out rather than analysed thinly.
