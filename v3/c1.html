04 — Method, record, rules

Built to be audited

  • AMethodology

    How a pick is made, what the model's number is, how edge is measured, how the record is kept.

  • BPublic track record

    Every pick is logged with outcome, odds and the closing line.

  • CResponsible use

    Educational analytics only. No guaranteed outcomes, no bookmaker activity. 18+.

■ 01Is SportBot AI a tipster service?+

No. A tipster sells a side and asks for trust. SportBot publishes rule-based picks against the pros' price, and every settled pick stays on the public record, losses included.

■ 02How do the predictions work?+

Two numbers sit on every match: the pros' price (Pinnacle, margin removed) and the model's number, in test. Where a licensed book pays more than the pros' price, that gap is the edge.

■ 03What is free?+

One pick a day, one match analysis, one price check and three AI questions a week. The record and the methodology are open to everyone.

■ 04What sports are covered?+

Major soccer competitions, NBA, EuroLeague, NFL, NHL, tennis, MLB with player props, and esports. A match without usable inputs is left out.
