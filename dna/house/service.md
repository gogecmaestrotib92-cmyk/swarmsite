# House structure: service landing pages

Section-by-section architecture of award-winning (or award-nominated) landing pages for service businesses, and a derived "house structure" for the page builder.

Method: sites located via WebSearch (Awwwards SOTD / Honorable Mention / nominee listings, 2024–2026); each homepage's text fetched with WebFetch on 2026-11-09 and recorded top to bottom. Visual types are inferred from alt text, filenames and markup where the fetch did not expose layout; those cases are marked "inferred". Navigation bars are not counted as sections. Word counts are approximate (visible body text incl. footer).

Replacements: Rally Interactive (rallyinteractive.com) fetched as a bare project index with no footer or contact text, so it is kept only as a note, not a full row set. Harper Grey LLP (harpergrey.com) fetched as fragmentary copy with no sections or footer and was replaced by two law firms that were nominated/featured on Awwwards in 2025: The Cook Group Legal and Kreisson. Awwwards listing pages for Joseph Law and South Cliff Dental Group were robots-blocked; the clinic slot is filled by LAVA dental (Awwwards Honorable Mention, 19 Jan 2026) and Clinique dentaire Gadoury (Awwwards nominee).

## Synthesis

### 1. Modal order of roles (8 full sites)

| Role | Sites | Share | Typical position |
|---|---|---|---|
| hero | 8 | 100 % | 1 |
| footer (with contact details) | 8 | 100 % | last |
| services / practice areas / features | 6 (Basement, Significa, Cook, Kreisson, LAVA, Gadoury) | 75 % | 2–3 (clinics, firms); after work (agencies) |
| about / people / culture block (any form) | 7 (all but Basement) | 88 % | middle |
| selected-work (incl. patient results) | 5 (Locomotive, Basement, Obys, Significa, LAVA) | 62 % | 1–2 for agencies; 5 for the clinic |
| contact section or CTA banner before footer | 5 (Basement, Cook, Kreisson, LAVA, Gadoury) | 62 % | second-to-last |
| clients-logos / badges / memberships | 4 (Basement, Significa, Cook, Kreisson) | 50 % | 2 (agency) or late (firms) |
| insights-blog / news | 4 (Locomotive, Significa, Cook, Kreisson) | 50 % | middle-late |
| team with photos | 2 (Cook, LAVA) | 25 % | middle |
| faq | 2 (Significa, LAVA) | 25 % | late, before contact |
| process / steps | 1–2 (LAVA intro; Kreisson's staged services) | 12–25 % | early |
| figures / stats section | 0 standalone; numbers embedded in 3 (Significa award counts, Kreisson "20 Years", Basement nav counts) | 0 % | — |
| testimonials / quote | 0 | 0 % | — |
| contact-form | 0 (Work & Co only, outside the 8) | 0 % | — |

Modal order (roles at ≥ 50 %): **hero → proof (work or logos) → services → about/people → insights → contact CTA → footer**. Agencies invert proof and services (work first, services after or never); firms and clinics put services first and proof later.

### 2. Optional roles and what sites do without content

- **Testimonials**: no site uses a client quote. Agencies substitute award badges and client logos; firms substitute certification badges and case-result headlines ("The Cook Group Secures Complete Summary Judgment Victory…"); clinics substitute patient-result photos (LAVA) or simply skip (Gadoury). Rule: skip, never fake.
- **Figures**: no stats grid anywhere. Numbers ride inside other beats: award counts beside logos (Significa), a "20 Years" badge in the hero and a "TWENTY YEARS" photo band (Kreisson), "Showcase(26)" / "Blog(29)" counts in nav (Basement). Substitute: one number in the hero or on the logo band.
- **Process**: only LAVA has a process intro, and it is photos, not numbered steps. Kreisson folds process into services ("From contract to completion" in three stages). Substitute: stage-ordered services.
- **Team**: real portraits only where people are the product (law, dentistry). Agencies substitute a manifesto paragraph (Locomotive, Obys) or culture/career cards (Significa).
- **FAQ**: appears only on the two longest pages (Significa 650 w, LAVA 1,600 w), both placed just before contact. Short pages skip it.
- **Insights**: present when the firm publishes; agencies show 3–6 items, firms show categories. Skipped on clinic pages.
- **Contact form**: skipped on every full-sample homepage; replaced by a CTA banner plus footer channels (see 4).

### 3. How proof is shown, and in what order

- **Work images first** for agencies: Locomotive (section 2, 5 projects), Significa (section 2, 3 projects with award badges), Obys/Rally (the grid is the page). Basement puts a 36-logo wall at section 2 and work at section 3.
- **Logos / badges** are the firm-side proof: Cook's 11 certification badges at section 3; Kreisson's 10 membership logos at section 7. Significa stacks award logos (with counts) next to services and client logos late (section 6).
- **Numbers** are never a standalone beat; they decorate a badge or logo (Significa, Kreisson).
- **Quotes**: none. Case-result news headlines (Cook) and patient photos (LAVA) are the quote substitutes.
- Order of proof types when several exist: agencies: work → logos (→ awards); firms: badges → results/news → team; clinic (LAVA): patient photos → team → price → FAQ.
- Visuals: work tiles and patient results are photos; firms rely on photos of people (Cook team, Kreisson photo bands, Gadoury clinic photo). Illustration appears only once (Work & Co tile, LAVA map).

### 4. Where contact sits

- **Header** CTA on 6/8 ("Let's talk", "Contact Us", "Get a quote", "TALK TO US", phone link). 
- **Pre-footer contact or CTA banner** on 5/8: Basement ("Contact" + mailto), Cook ("Ready to Meet Your Trusted Litigation Attorneys?" → button), Kreisson ("CONTACT US" → "GET IN TOUCH"), LAVA (location block + repeated "Make an appointment"), Gadoury (CTA banner + address/hours/phone block).
- **Footer** carries address/phone/email on 6/8 (Locomotive, Obys, Kreisson, LAVA, Cook, Work & Co).
- **Form**: none of the 8 full sites; Work & Co is the lone real form (7 fields, mid-page). Everything else is mailto (Basement, Obys, Kreisson, LAVA), tel (Cook, Gadoury, LAVA, Kreisson), or a link to a contact page / quote page (Locomotive, Significa, Cook, Kreisson). Booking is a tel link (Gadoury) or appointment link (LAVA, Kreisson "BOOK A CONSULTATION").

### 5. Recommended house structure (service landings)

Beat types from {features, photo, steps, cells, quote, faq, form, logos}.

| Slot | Name | Beat | Visual | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | features (single statement) + optional cells (one number) | editorial type; optional background photo/video (clinics, firms) | One sentence of what you are, one CTA; add a single proof token (award badge, "20 years") if real. |
| 2 | Proof of work | photo (work / results) | 3–6 project or result images with one-line caption | Comes directly after the hero for agencies; if no work images exist, substitute logos (slot 5 moves here) — never an empty slot. |
| 3 | Services | features | 3–6 cards; clinics 4, agencies 4 groups, firms 3 stages | Clinics and firms may swap slots 2 and 3 (services first). Order items as stages if the service is sequential (replaces a separate process beat). |
| 4 | People / about | photo (people) | one photo band or portraits + ≤ 60-word paragraph | Portraits when people are the product (law, medical); manifesto paragraph for studios. Optional CTA to About/Careers. |
| 5 | Credentials | logos (+ cells) | 6–12 client, award, certification or membership logos; optional counts | Second proof; the only place for numbers. Skip if fewer than 4 real logos. |
| 6 | Insights / FAQ | faq (or features for 3 posts) | none | Optional; include FAQ only on long-consideration services (clinics, consultancies, product studios); include posts only if there are ≥ 3 recent. Sits just before contact. |
| 7 | Contact | form (short) or CTA banner | none | One headline, one button; show the direct channels (email, tel, booking) beside it. Form only if the business actually triages leads; default is mailto/booking. |
| 8 | Footer | logos (wordmark) | none | Address, phone, email, hours (clinics), social, legal. Contact details must appear here even if slot 7 exists. |

Quote beat: not in the house default; allowed as an optional insert after slot 2 only when a verbatim client quote exists.

### Comparison with the draft

DRAFT: hero(editorial type) → services (features cards) → work (photo + one sentence of proof) → process (steps) → figures (cells) → voice (one quote) → faq → contact (form).

Keep:
- **Hero as editorial type** — 5/8 heroes are typographic (Locomotive, Basement, Significa, Kreisson, Cook's text over video); clinics add a photo.
- **Services as feature cards** — 6/8 have them, with 3–6 top-level items.
- **Work as photo + one line** — matches Locomotive ("Featured work", 5), Significa ("Selected work.", 3), LAVA ("Our patients' smiles speak for themselves.", ~9).
- **FAQ before contact** — matches Significa and LAVA placement.
- **Contact last** — a pre-footer contact/CTA block appears on 5/8.

Change:
- **Swap services and work for agencies.** Work precedes services in every agency that has both (Basement, Significa); Locomotive and Obys show work and no services at all. Make proof slot 2 and services slot 3, with an allowed swap for clinics/firms (Gadoury, Cook, Kreisson, LAVA all lead with services or features).
- **Demote process (steps) from a fixed slot to an option inside services.** Only LAVA has a process intro, and it is photographic; Kreisson expresses process as staged services. A numbered steps beat is not evidenced on any awarded page.
- **Remove standalone figures (cells).** 0/8 sites have a stats row. Numbers appear only as a hero badge or beside logos; fold cells into slot 1 or slot 5.
- **Replace "voice (one quote)" with "people/about + logos".** 0/8 sites show a testimonial; 7/8 have an about/people block and 4/8 a logo/badge band. Quote becomes an optional insert.
- **Add a credentials/logos slot.** Missing from the draft but present on 50 % of sites and the main proof for law firms.
- **Make the contact form optional, default to CTA banner + direct channels.** 0/8 awarded homepages carry a form; they use mailto, tel, booking link or a link to a contact page, with full details in the footer. Keep the form variant for lead-triage businesses (Work & Co pattern).
- **Add the footer as an explicit slot** carrying contact details; 6/8 sites put address/phone/email there, and for Locomotive and Obys it is the only contact point.
- **Length guidance**: agencies 400–650 words / 6–10 sections; firms 1,000–1,800 words / 9–10 sections; clinics 425–1,600 words / 7–11 sections. The house default should produce ~6–8 sections and ~500 words, extensible to 10 for firms and clinics.
