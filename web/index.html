Questions

Does this change my real website?

No. We only read it. You get a preview on our side; your site stays exactly as it is until you decide.

What if my photos are bad?

The preview uses images found on your site. Image quality and relevance vary, so review the result before using it.

How long does it take?

Five to fifteen minutes. It's real work: reading your site, choosing a concept, setting type, placing photos, checking every screen on a phone.

Does it rebuild all my pages?

The current preview focuses on a landing page and can link to pages on your original site. Review the content check and your preview for anything that needs attention.

Can I use the design in my existing project?

A kit for developers using Claude Code and Codex is in development. The current experience lets you explore the redesign preview.
