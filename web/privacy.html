Privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026 · draft for review by a lawyer, written in plain words.

What we collect

The URL you paste, and what our software reads from that public site (text, images, colours). If you buy, your email and payment confirmation from our payment provider; we never see your card number. Basic, cookieless analytics on this site.

Why

To build your preview, deliver what you bought, and send receipts. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not send marketing email unless you opt in.

Who processes it

Hosting (Vercel), payments (Stripe), email delivery, and AI providers that generate parts of the rebuild. Each receives only what it needs.

Your rights

Ask us to show, correct or delete your data at any time: hello@swarmsite.si. Previews are deleted 30 days after creation unless you keep them.

Cookies

Only what is needed to keep you signed in and to remember your choices. No tracking cookies load before you agree.
