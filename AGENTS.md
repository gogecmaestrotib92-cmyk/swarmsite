# AGENTS.md — rules for anyone (human or model) landing code in this repo

## The step before you land code
Every significant change goes through, in this order, before `git commit`:

1. **Live test** — not "it compiles". Run `scripts/livetest.sh`. It renders the sample direction through every blueprint, runs the contract (`lib/qa/contract.mjs`) on each render at 1440 and 390, and fails if any render errors or any contract scores below 75. When the change touches the server, capture or the swarm, also run a real job through `https://swarmsite.si/app` (or `POST /jobs`) in the browser and read `critic.json`, `contract.json` and `swarm.json` for that job — the trace, not the vibe.
2. **Autoreview** — read the full diff (`git diff --staged`) as a reviewer who did not write it: what breaks on a site with no photos, on a phone, on a dark band, with a 9-word h1? Fix before committing, never after.
3. **Commit** — one change per commit, message says what the page does differently, not what the code does.
4. **Track** — append one line to `REFACTOR.md` under today's date: what changed, the live-test result, the next bottleneck.

## Broken layout never ships (added 2026-11-10)
`lib/qa/layout.mjs <page.html>` measures every heading and paragraph at 390, 768, 1100, 1440 and 1920 and names what is broken as typesetting: a heading stacked a word to a line, a split word, a line of punctuation alone, a paragraph in a sliver of a column, text clipped or lying over other text. It is not a matter of taste and it is not weighed against the critic. `scripts/livetest.sh` fails on any break in any blueprint. In the loop (`lib/swarm/run.py mend_layout`) a composed section with a break goes back to what it was before composition, and a composed page whose first screen is broken is refused. A new kind of breakage seen on a real page becomes a check in `layout.mjs` with a fixture in `tests/layout-breaks.mjs` — never a patch for that one page. Check widths between and beyond the two the contract uses: the column that started this was fine at 1440 and 186 px wide at 1800.

## The landing is the engine's first client (added 2026-11-11, rebuilt 2026-11-12)
`web/index.html` is held to what a client's page is held to, and is built the way a client's page now is: on a template of the library — its measured build (sizes, spacing, type scale, radii, buttons, motion) with our words swapped in (owner, 2026-11-12). The shared layer of every page is `web/css/site.css` (tokens, type, buttons, nav box, fields); the landing's sections are `web/css/landing.css`; its movement is the template's own script, `web/js/landing.js` — no second motion library beside it. The one thing on it that is not the template's is the product's own agents (`web/js/swarm2.js`) on the stage: named cursors acting on the places `pageEvents()` (`server/templates.mjs`) measured on a made-up client's page (`web/visuals/ui-page.webp`, `ui-page.json`) — the same script, fed the same kind of events, as the working screen. An agent only draws what an event reports; with no event of its own it may frame a place an event already named, without words (`tests/swarm-agents.mjs`). The product's header (`web/header.css`) is the same nav box. There is one way into a job from every entry: `POST /jobs` with `options.engine='templates'` (an address; an address with `options.screens`, the visitor's screenshots sent to `POST /uploads`; or the `/start` form with `options.source='form'`) — the old brief form is gone. The editor's tabs and its download dialog are the UI SDK's elements (`web/vendor/ui-sdk`, Vercel's `@ui-sdk/web`, vendored as its `browser` folder, a beta: see the README there) — keyboard, focus and ARIA are the library's, the look is ours; a new control of that kind (a menu, a popover, drag and drop) is taken from it before it is written by hand. "Desktop" in the editor is the page 1440 wide made smaller to fit; the place on the page is kept when it is drawn again; a check that could not run is said as that, never as the visitor's edit. The editor (`web/edit.html`) is the page first: it fills the screen and a text is typed where it stands; one panel with four tabs (Text, Pictures, Colours, Sections) shows one thing at a time, the texts as a short list of the page's parts; the download is one button that opens its steps (publishing at a swarmsite.si name is built, `server/publish.mjs`, and not offered: no page says "publish" or promises an address, and the editor shows it only where `config.js` sets `SWARMSITE_PUBLISH` — owner, 2026-11-13: hosting is not a product yet); on a phone the tabs are a bar at the foot and the panel a sheet (`tests/edit-page.mjs`). The form (`web/start.html`) asks a little at a time: four short steps and a last look, one on the screen; only the name is needed — and, of software or a phone app, two screenshots of the product, said at the first step and not sent without (2026-11-15); the kind of business comes first and shapes what is asked; what the page will have is said beside the steps and before it is made; the data sent is unchanged (`tests/start-form.mjs`). The dashboard (`web/dashboard.html`) lists a visitor's projects as cards in the chooser's manner — the covers of its designs, its name, where it stands, and "Open designs" / "Edit" — and starts a project by the same three ways in; what it is told about a job is `mine()` (`server/auth.mjs`): design ids, covers, the paid design, never a template's name or a private file. The editor's check reads the gate through `runGate` (`server/editor.mjs`): the gate exits 1 when a page fails and that is its answer, not a failure of the tool. The colours the editor offers on a design are those of a tone the job measured that design to hold (`tones`, `lib/templates/job.py`; `offered`, `server/editor.mjs`): a design is gated on the client's own ground, and once more on the other tone before its colours are offered — never offered unmeasured (2026-11-14). In the chooser the line above each card says where that design stands; nothing says "ready" for all three while one is not. Never name the reference a template was rebuilt from anywhere in `web/`. `tests/test_landing.py` fails when a fact the page states is no longer what the code does, when the three ways in or the address field move from the opening, or when a page names a reference; `tests/landing-layout.mjs` is its gate (layout at five widths, headline by 1.5 s, nothing hidden, no spill, links/field/menu answering, readable words); before a change to it lands, serve `web/` and run `node lib/qa/template_gate.mjs <its address> --no-more`, which judges it on pixels at 1440 and 390 (no test runs this for you). What it shows of our work is our own interface photographed with a made-up client of `tests/fixtures/templates/` (`web/visuals/ui-*.webp`: the working screen, the chooser, the editor, one finished design) — never a real client's page until the owner calls one worth showing (2026-11-11, 2026-11-13). Scripts and stylesheets of `web/` are never cached for days (`web/vercel.json`, `tests/test_web_cache.py`).

## What search reads is written from one place (added 2026-11-18)
`scripts/seo.py` writes the blog (`web/blog/*.html`, `feed.xml`) from `content/blog/<slug>.html`, the sitemap, and the structured data of `web/index.html` — read off the page's own FAQ and price, so it cannot say what the page does not. An article is its text with a JSON comment of facts at the top; never edit a page of `web/blog` by hand. After a change to an article, to the landing's FAQ, price or description: `python3 scripts/seo.py` (`--cards` when a title or a picture changed: it photographs the 1200×630 sharing cards of `web/og/`, `scripts/og_cards.mjs`). When the words of the landing, privacy or terms change, set that page's day in `PAGES` there. A new public page gets a title, a description, a canonical address and a line in `PAGES`; a page kept out of search keeps `noindex` and stays out of the sitemap. `tests/test_seo.py` holds all of this, and that `web/llms.txt` quotes the landing's price; `tests/blog-layout.mjs` is the blog's gate (layout at five widths, a phone, links, the field, the menu). The blog is on the shared layer (`web/css/site.css`) with `web/css/blog.css`; its pictures are our own interface's screens. A guide says of the product only what the landing says; says of another company's service only what its own documentation says, with the source in the article's `sources`; and claims no rating, review or number we do not have.

## Who came is counted by us, and read by the owner alone (added 2026-11-19)
`server/track.mjs` writes what leaves no row anywhere else to the table `events` (`server/migrations/20261119_events.sql`): a page opened (`web/js/track.js` → `POST /e`, on every way in and on the blog through `scripts/seo.py`), a design opened full screen, a job refused (limit of an address, full queue, day's ceiling), an edit, edits kept, a payment started, a download. No third party's script, no cookie, nothing kept in a visitor's browser, never an address or a whole referrer: a visitor is a key that is made new each day. Recording never waits and never throws — a write that fails is counted and dropped. The owner's page is `web/admin.html` (Serbian, at `/admin`, `noindex`, linked from nowhere — `tests/owner-tracking.mjs` fails on a link to it); its numbers come from `GET /admin/stats` (`server/admin.mjs`), which answers only to the deployment's `ADMIN_TOKEN` and to nobody when it is not set. It shows counts of rows that exist — jobs, failures and payments from `jobs`, the rest from `events` — and "nema podataka" where nothing was recorded, never a zero that was not counted; a failed job's reason passes `scrub()`. A new step worth counting is a new event name there, never a second analytics tool.
Visitors are the people who are not us. A browser that has opened `/admin` keeps reporting, marked as ours (`o: 1` → `detail = own`); its key of the day is ours, and so is every job started under it (`jobs.options.vk`, the day's key of who started the job, set by the server and kept out of every public file) and every step on such a job. The owner's tiles and funnel count visitors only; our visits and our jobs, and jobs no page was seen opening for (scripts, tests, a blocked counter), are said apart in words — never added in.

## Effort
Use high effort for anything in `lib/render`, `lib/swarm`, `lib/qa`, `server/`. A cheap pass that "looks fine" has cost us a day more than once (sticky that never stuck, text invisible on dark bands, the same picture four times).

## What the loop is (see LOOPS.md)
Deterministic renderer + blueprints = the engine. Agents only write `direction.json`. The critic grades taste; the contract grades promises. Neither is allowed to edit the page directly.

## Deploys kill runs
Railway restarts the worker on every push; a job that was running is lost (the worker now marks it failed on boot). Before `git push`, check nothing is running: `GET https://swarmsite-production.up.railway.app/jobs?status=running` — or accept that you are killing it and say so.

## Never
- Never paste secrets into code, logs or chat. Keys live in Railway variables; the owner sets them.
- Never add a model call inside the renderer.
- Never fix a critic finding by special-casing one site; fix the rule that let it through.

## Working in parallel (added 2026-11-08)
`main` is the deployed branch. Any agent other than the owner's active session works on a branch and opens a PR. Before every commit: `npm test` (pytest + every `tests/*.mjs`) and `scripts/livetest.sh 80` must both pass. A test that a change breaks is fixed or consciously updated in the same commit — never left red.
